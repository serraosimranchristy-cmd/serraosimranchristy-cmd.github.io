About Me

I am a dual-degree master's candidate bridging the gap between biological sciences and computer science. My research focuses on computational systems biology, network dynamics, and applying advanced Natural Language Processing (NLP) to psychometric and clinical data.

Currently, I am investigating how social network structures influence empathy in high-pressure STEM environments, and developing machine learning tools to identify early-warning biomarkers for neurological transitions.
